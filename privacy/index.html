PRIVACY POLICY — FAMILY FINANCE PLANNER Last updated: 10 November 2026 Nisnil AI-Finance Solutions ("we") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect when you buy and use Family Finance Planner. What we collect - When you buy: your name, email address (the Gmail you use for FamilyFi), phone number and payment reference. Payments are processed by our payment gateway; we do not receive your card or bank details. - When you contact support: the messages and screenshots you send us. - Your plan: your Gmail address, plan, start and end dates and payment reference, stored in our app's settings so it knows whose plan is active. What we do NOT collect - Your transactions, balances, budgets, investments, loans, insurance details, family details or documents. These are saved only in your own Google Drive, by the app running in your browser. They never pass through our servers. (Customers of the earlier offline edition keep their data only in their own file.) How we use your information - To deliver the product, activate your licence, send invoices, provide support and send important product updates. - We do not sell or rent your personal information. Google account access When you tap "Continue with Google", the app asks permission to see your name and email address (to know whose plan it is) and to create and use only the files FamilyFi itself creates in your Google Drive (Google's "drive.file" permission). It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive. To check your plan, the app sends your sign-in token to our plan server, which asks Google for your email address and does not store the token. You can remove it any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. For live prices the app also keeps a small Google Sheet in that folder that looks up share prices with Google Finance, and downloads AMFI's public mutual fund NAV list. No personal data is sent to AMFI, to Google Finance or to us. Optional encryption If you turn on the passphrase lock, your data is encrypted on your device before it is saved. Your passphrase and recovery code are never sent to us, and we cannot unlock or recover your data. Website analytics and cookies Our website nisnil.co.in uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits, pages viewed, the country and type of device, and where visitors came from. It sets cookies only if you tap "Accept" on the cookie banner; if you decline, no analytics cookies are set. IP addresses are not stored by Google Analytics 4. We use these figures only to improve the website. You can change your choice any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. The FamilyFi app itself (familyfi.app.nisnil.co.in) does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Retention and your rights In line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its Rules: - Access, correction, deletion: write to familyfi.app@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days. - Withdraw consent at any time: remove FamilyFi's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions and email us. We then delete your plan record within 30 days, keeping only the payment records that tax law requires us to keep. - Data breach: if a breach ever affects your personal data, we will inform you and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires. - If we do not resolve your complaint, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India. Security We keep customer lists in access-controlled accounts and share them only with service providers needed to run the business (for example the payment gateway and email service).
