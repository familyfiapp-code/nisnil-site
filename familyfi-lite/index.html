Questions

How do I install it?

Open the app link on your phone. Android (Chrome): menu ⋮ → Add to Home screen. iPhone (Safari): Share → Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app.

Does it work without internet?

No. FamilyFi Lite loads and saves your data straight from your own Google Drive, so it needs the internet. That is also why nothing is left on the phone if you lose it.

How do I pay?

Tap Buy in the app (More → Your plan) or on this page. Pay on Razorpay with the same Gmail you use in the app; you come back to the app and your plan date updates. There is no auto-debit.

Can I move to full FamilyFi later?

Yes. Buy a FamilyFi plan, open FamilyFi with the same Gmail, and bring in your Lite entries and goals with one tap.

Can I get a refund?

Please read our refund & cancellation policy. The 15-day free trial lets you try everything first.
