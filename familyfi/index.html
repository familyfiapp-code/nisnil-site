User manual

Read chapters 1–4 on day one. Come back to the rest when you need it.

1Before you start
  • A Gmail account, and Chrome, Edge or Safari on your phone or laptop.
  • Keep ready: your bank accounts, credit cards and cash wallets, with the last 4 digits of each, and one recent statement per account.
  • Optional: your mutual fund CAS statement, loan statement and insurance policy details.
The planner never needs passwords, PINs, OTPs, CVV or full card numbers.
2Sign in and start your free trial
  1. Tap Start 15-day free trial. The planner opens at familyfi.app.nisnil.co.in.
  2. Tap Continue with Google and choose your Gmail. Use the same Gmail later when you pay, so your plan attaches to your data.
  3. Google asks once to let FamilyFi save its own files in your Drive. Tap Continue. FamilyFi cannot see your other files.
  4. Your trial end date shows at the bottom of every page. Seven days before it ends, a reminder with renewal links appears.
  5. On your phone, tap Add to Home screen in the browser menu.
3Set up your planner (5 minutes, once)
  1. Type a planner name, for example Sharma Family, and add family members.
  2. Add each bank account, credit card and cash wallet with its opening balance.
  3. Tap Create my planner. 74 ready-made Indian household categories are added; rename or add your own later.
  4. Open My accounts › edit account and type the last 4 digits of each account and card.

Want to look around first? Tap Explore with sample data, and clear it later under Accounts & categories › Planner.

5Recording money

A. Import a statement (fastest)

  1. Open Import statement, pick the account and drop in the Excel, CSV or PDF file. For password PDFs, type the password when asked.
  2. For Excel/CSV, confirm the column matching once; it is remembered.
  3. Review the rows. Rows already recorded are skipped, so overlapping statements never double count.
  4. Tap Import. Untagged rows wait under Transactions › Show: To review.

B. Add from a bank SMS (10 seconds)

  1. Copy the debit or credit SMS, open Transactions → ✉ Add from SMS and paste.
  2. Check and tap Add this SMS. The statement line later replaces it, so nothing counts twice.

C. Transfers

Card bill payments, bank-to-bank transfers and cash withdrawals are not spending. Use ⇄ Transfer between accounts, or tag a row Card Payment or Bank Transfer.

D. Cash and one-off entries

Use Add transaction. A row with the wrong sign can be flipped with ⇄.

6Budgets and plans
  • Type figures into the Budget grid. Fill › copies one month to the rest of the year.
  • Compare shows budget versus actual; Expense trends highlights categories that keep rising.
  • Plan & funds shows bills still due and expected savings for 12 months.
  • Cards & cash flow lists each card’s billing cycle and due date.
7Investments, loans and insurance
  • Mutual funds: type part of the name and pick the scheme; units from your CAS. Online, NAV updates daily.
  • Shares: company name or NSE symbol (BSE-only: 6-digit code). Not found? Use a manual price.
  • FD, PPF, property: add the amount and dates; they count in Net worth.
  • Loans: outstanding principal, rate and EMI; try a prepayment to see interest saved and the new end date.
  • Insurance: cover, premium and next due date; tap Paid to roll to next year; attach the policy PDF.
8Lock with a passphrase (optional)
  1. Open Home › Privacy & security, type a passphrase of 8+ characters twice and tap Turn on encryption.
  2. Write down the recovery code and tap I have saved it.
  3. It asks for the passphrase each time and locks after 15 minutes idle. Forgot it? Use the recovery code.
Nobody, including us, can open your data without the passphrase or recovery code.
9Backups
  • Every change saves to your Drive within seconds; daily backups are kept for 30 days. Accounts & categories › Backup to back up now, restore or export to Excel.
  • Good habit: export an Excel copy once a month.

  • If a plan ends, your data stays safe in your Drive and opens again when you renew.
  • Spouse or family: open familyfi.app.nisnil.co.in on any device and sign in with the same Google account.
  • “Changed on another device”: tap Reload latest.

Questions families ask

Is my financial data safe?
Your data is saved only in your own Google Drive. We cannot open it. You can also lock it with a passphrase (AES-256).
Do I have to connect my bank account?
No. You never share bank passwords. You import statements (Excel, CSV, PDF) or paste bank SMS yourself.
What does the Google permission allow?
Only files FamilyFi creates in your Drive (its data file, backups and documents you upload). It cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes. FamilyFi works in any phone or laptop browser, with the same data everywhere. Use “Add to Home screen” to open it like an app.
What happens after the 15-day trial?
Choose Monthly or Yearly to continue. If you don’t, the app pauses and your data stays safely in your Drive for when you come back.
Can my spouse use it too?
Yes. The planner is built for the whole family: members, their policies, investments and accounts. One plan covers one Google account; use the same login on your spouse’s phone, or ask us about a second account.
How do I pay?
Securely through Razorpay: UPI, cards, net banking or wallets. Use the Gmail you use for FamilyFi. See refund policy.
Is this investment advice?
No. The planner organises and calculates your own information. It does not recommend products, returns or tax decisions.
