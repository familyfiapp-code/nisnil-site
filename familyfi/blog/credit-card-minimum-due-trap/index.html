Paying only the minimum due? Here is what it costs

Raju & Priya · Episode 12 · 13 November 2026 · 2 min read

After Diwali shopping the card bill is ₹25,000 and Raju plans to pay only ₹1,200. Priya explains the interest.

The story

RajuThe card bill after Diwali shopping is ₹25,000. I’ll just pay the minimum: ₹1,200.
PriyaWait. The rest is charged about 3.5% a month. That is roughly 42% a year!
PriyaChange the budget a little and pay the full bill.
Save this rule

Card rule · pay in full, before the due date

  • on timeFull bill
  • pay as much as you canIf not possible

Why it works

The minimum due keeps your card in good standing, but it does not stop interest. Interest is charged on the unpaid balance, and usually on new purchases too until the balance is cleared.

Card interest is commonly around 3–3.75% a month, which is over 40% a year. No investment reliably earns that, so clearing a card balance is one of the best “returns” a family can get.

If you cannot pay in full this month, pay as much as possible, stop new card spends, and clear it within one or two cycles. A cheaper personal loan to close card debt can also help.

Do it this week

  1. Know each card’s statement and due date.
  2. Budget the full bill before the due date.
  3. Pay in full; if not, pay the most you can.
  4. Pause card spending until it is cleared.
