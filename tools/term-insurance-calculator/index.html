Why a needs-based number

“Ten times your income” is quick but ignores loans, the number of years your family depends on you, and what you already have. This calculator adds up three needs and subtracts your existing cover and savings.

  • Living expenses for the years your family needs support, growing with inflation.
  • Loans that would otherwise fall on your family.
  • Children’s goals such as higher education.

Choosing the policy

  • A pure term plan gives the most cover for the premium.
  • Cover until your youngest child is independent and loans are paid, often age 60–65.
  • Tell your family where the policy is, and name a nominee.

Questions

Should employer life cover count?

Count it only if you are sure it continues; it usually ends when you leave the job.

Does this include health insurance?

No. Health cover is separate. Check that every family member has it.

Is this financial advice?

No. It is a calculation from your inputs. Speak to a licensed adviser for product choices.
