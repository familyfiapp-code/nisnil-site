How it works

  1. Your goal’s cost today is grown by inflation to the year you need it.
  2. What you have already saved is grown at the expected return and subtracted.
  3. The monthly SIP that closes the gap is worked out, with the SIP rising every year by the step-up you choose.

Why step up

Your income usually rises every year. Raising the SIP by 10% a year means a much smaller first SIP for the same goal. In Raju & Priya’s half-half rule, half of every increment goes to the SIP.

Questions

What inflation should I use?

Education costs in India have often risen faster than general inflation; 8–10% is a cautious figure for education, 6% for general goals.

What return should I assume?

Use a conservative figure for your fund type. Equity returns vary year to year and are not guaranteed.

Can I track the goal later?

Yes. FamilyFi’s Goals show how far each goal has come, next to your SIPs and net worth.
