How it works

  1. Sign in with Google at tax.nisnil.co.in. No new password to remember.
  2. Upload your documents: Form 16, bank interest certificates, investment proofs, capital gains statements. You can also fill in the figures you know. No payment needed yet.
  3. We prepare your computation, comparing the old and new tax regimes, and send it to you.
  4. You approve it (or tell us what to fix), then pay by UPI QR or payment link.
  5. We file your return. You e-verify with an Aadhaar OTP, and your ITR-V and computation appear in your account.

Documents to keep ready

  • PAN, Aadhaar, and the mobile number linked to them
  • Form 16 from each employer
  • Bank account details for the refund (IFSC and account number)
  • Interest certificates (savings, FD), and AIS / 26AS if you have them
  • Investment and insurance proofs if you choose the old regime (80C, 80D, HRA rent receipts, home-loan interest)
  • Capital gains statement from your broker or mutual fund registrar

Good to know

  • Your documents are stored in our Google Drive and used only to prepare and file your return.
  • We file only after you approve the computation and the payment is confirmed.
  • Tax audit cases and certificates that need a Chartered Accountant are handled through an associate CA, quoted separately.
  • Questions? Email familyfi.app@gmail.com with “ITR”.
